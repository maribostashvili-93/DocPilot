## 1.0 Welcome: The Game That Rewards Smart Moves

### What You're About to Play

Minescape is a crash-and-hold mining game where **you control the pace, manage the risk, and decide when to cash out**. Unlike games where the house decides everything, Minescape puts the outcome in your hands: reveal safe squares and watch your potential winnings grow. But beware—hidden mines lurk beneath the surface.

#### The Core Loop

1. **Set Your Bet** — Choose your stake
2. **Pick Your Grid** — Configure board size and mine count
3. **Reveal Safely** — Click safe squares and watch multipliers climb
4. **Cash Out on Your Terms** — Lock in winnings before hitting a mine (or risk it all for more)

That's it. Simple rules. Infinite strategies.

## 2.0 Getting Started: Your First Hand

### Step 1: Understanding the Play Screen

When you load Minescape, you'll see a clean, confidence-inspiring interface designed for fast decision-making:

**Top Bar**
- Your current balance displayed prominently (e.g., "960,873.84 GEL")
- Menu icon for account and game settings
- Quick access to your betting history

**The Bet Configuration Panel**
- **Bet Amount Input** — Set your stake. Use the preset buttons (1x, 2x, Max) for quick adjustments

## 3.0 Placing Your Bet: The Foundation

### Setting Bet Amount

Your bet is the money in play. Everything earned in a round is a multiple of this amount.

#### Example Math

- **Your Bet:** 100 GEL
- **Current Multiplier:** 2.5x
- **Your Potential Winnings:** 250 GEL
- **Your Profit:** 150 GEL (250 – 100)

#### Quick Preset Buttons

- **÷2** — Cut your last bet in half (useful for recovery rounds)
- **2X** — Double your last bet (raise stakes on a hot streak)
- **Max** — Go all-in with your entire balance (high risk, high reward)

**Bankroll Management Tip:** Don't bet it all on the first hand. Minescape rewards patience and consistency. A 100-GEL steady bet over 10 rounds beats a 1000-GEL all-in that ends in disaster.

### Confirming Your Setup

Before you can start, your configuration must be valid:

- ✅ Bet amount is set and within limits
- ✅ Grid size is selected
- ✅ Mine count is compatible with grid size
- ✅ You have sufficient balance

When all checks pass, the green **Start Autobet** button lights up. You're ready to play.

## 4.0 The Core Gameplay: Reveal and React

### How a Round Works

#### Moment 1: The Board Appears

After you hit Start, your grid materializes. All tiles are closed (shown as crates). The board layout is randomized—the mines are already placed, but hidden from you.

**You now have complete control.** No timers. No pressure. This is your decision to make.

#### Moment 2: Your First Reveal

Click any closed tile. The game executes your choice:

- **You hit a safe tile?** ✅ The tile flips to show a green checkmark. Your **multiplier increases**. The board stays open.
- **You hit a mine?** ❌ Game over. Your round ends. Your bet is lost.

#### Moment 3: Growing Your Multiplier

Each safe reveal adds to your multiplier. The exact increment depends on:

- Grid size (more total tiles = smaller increments)
- Number of mines (fewer mines = smaller increments, more mines = bigger increments)
- How many safe tiles you've already revealed

**Example progression (25-tile grid, 1 mine):**
- After 1 reveal: 1.04x
- After 3 reveals: 1.13x
- After 5 reveals: 1.25x
- After 8 reveals: 1.50x
- After 12 reveals: 2.10x

#### Moment 4: The Crucial Decision

With each reveal, your potential winnings grow. But so does your risk.

**You have three choices:**

1. **Keep Playing** — Reveal another tile, push for a higher multiplier
2. **Cash Out** — Lock in your winnings right now, walk away a winner
3. **Let It Ride** — In autobet mode, let the system reveal tiles for you

**This is the heart of Minescape.** Greed vs. caution. One more click could double your winnings—or erase everything.

## 5.0 Cashing Out: Locking in Your Win

### The Cash-Out Decision

The moment you've been working toward is here. Your multiplier is climbing. Maybe you're at 2.5x. Maybe you're at 8x. Maybe you're tempted to go even higher.

**How to Cash Out**

1. Click the orange **CASH OUT** button (it appears during active play)
2. Confirm your intention (in manual mode, this locks it in immediately)
3. Your winnings are deposited to your balance instantly

**What You Receive**

Your bet × Your current multiplier = Your payout

**Example:**
- Bet: 500 GEL
- Current Multiplier: 3.5x
- Cash-Out Amount: 1,750 GEL
- Your Profit: 1,250 GEL

### Timing the Perfect Cash-Out

Timing separates legends from lessons. Consider these scenarios:

#### The Cautious Player

- Bets 100 GEL
- Hits 1.5x after 2 safe reveals
- Cashes out immediately
- **Profit: 50 GEL**
- Low risk, low reward, but sustainable

#### The Aggressive Player

- Bets 100 GEL
- Hits 2.0x after 3 reveals
- Hits 3.5x after 5 reveals
- Keeps going...
- Hits 6.2x after 8 reveals
- Finally cashes out
- **Profit: 520 GEL**
- Higher risk, but rewarded for patience

#### The Gambler

- Bets 100 GEL
- Hits 5.0x after 6 reveals
- "One more won't hurt..."
- **Hits a mine**
- **Result: -100 GEL**
- Thrilling, but heartbreaking

### AutoCash-Out (Advanced Feature)

For experienced players, set an auto cash-out target:
- Set a multiplier goal (e.g., 3.0x)
- When the multiplier hits your target, Minescape cashes out automatically
- Removes emotion. Locks in consistent wins.

## 6.0 Auto Mode: Let the Game Play Itself

### What Is AutoBet?

AutoBet lets you set Minescape on a loop. Configure rules once, and the game executes them repeatedly, round after round.

**Ideal for:**
- Consistent, disciplined play
- Longer sessions without constant clicking
- Testing betting strategies
- Afk-friendly gameplay

### Setting Up AutoBet

In the AutoBet tab, configure:

1. **Number of Rounds** — How many hands to play before stopping (e.g., 10, 100, 1000)
2. **Bet Amount per Round** — Fixed amount or varied (e.g., double on loss)
3. **Auto Cash-Out Target** — Multiplier to automatically cash out at (e.g., 2.5x)
4. **Grid & Mines** — Stay consistent or vary per round
5. **Stop Conditions**
   - Stop after X rounds
   - Stop if balance drops below a threshold
   - Stop if a single round wins exceed a target
   - Stop on first loss

### AutoBet in Action

**Example Session:**
- **Setup:** 10 rounds, 100-GEL bets, auto cash-out at 2.0x
- **Round 1:** Hit mine immediately (-100 GEL) — next round doubles to 200 GEL
- **Round 2:** Hit 2.0x, auto-cash-out (+200 GEL, still -100 net)
- **Round 3:** Hit 3.5x, wait... auto cash-out was 2.0x, so (+200 GEL, now +100 net)
- **Round 4–10:** Continue pattern...

After 10 rounds, you've automated the grind. No need to stare at the screen.

### Smart AutoBet Strategies

#### The Martingale Approach (Double on Loss)

- Lose a hand? Double your bet next round.
- Win a hand? Return to base bet.
- **Danger:** Requires a large bankroll. One long losing streak can wipe you out.

#### The Flat Bet (Consistency)

- Same bet every round
- Small, sustainable profits compound over time
- **Advantage:** Predictable, low stress, mathematically sound for long-term play

#### The Loss Recovery Spiral

- Start with 50-GEL bets
- On loss, increase to 75 GEL
- On another loss, go to 100 GEL
- Once you win a round, drop back to 50 GEL
- **Goal:** Gradual recovery without risking bankroll explosion

## 7.0 Understanding the Board: Grid Sizes and Odds

### The Mathematics Behind the Game

Minescape isn't purely luck. The grid size, mine count, and your reveals create calculable odds.

#### Grid Mechanics

**Before Your First Click**
- Total tiles: Determined by grid size (25, 36, 49, or 64)
- Mine positions: Randomized and locked in when you hit Start
- Your odds of hitting a mine on first click: (Number of Mines) ÷ (Total Tiles)

**Example: 25-tile grid, 3 mines**
- Probability of mine on first reveal: 3/25 = 12%
- Probability of safe tile: 22/25 = 88%

**After Each Safe Reveal**
- Your odds improve (fewer unopened tiles, same number of mines)
- Multiplier increments increase (fewer tiles = bigger jumps)

**Example Progression (49-tile grid, 5 mines, bet of 100 GEL)**

| Reveals | Safe Remaining | Multiplier | Win if Cash | Risk Next |
|---------|----------------|-----------|------------|-----------|
| 0 | 44 | 1.00x | 100 | 10.2% (5/49) |
| 1 | 43 | 1.09x | 109 | 11.6% (5/44) |
| 3 | 41 | 1.30x | 130 | 12.2% (5/41) |
| 5 | 39 | 1.56x | 156 | 12.8% (5/39) |
| 10 | 34 | 2.40x | 240 | 14.7% (5/34) |
| 15 | 29 | 3.85x | 385 | 17.2% (5/29) |
| 20 | 24 | 6.50x | 650 | 20.8% (5/24) |

**Key insight:** Each safe reveal makes the *next* reveal riskier. The higher your multiplier, the more you stand to lose.

### Optimal Reveal Strategy

#### Conservative Play (Beginners)

- Target 4–6 reveals per round
- Average multiplier: 1.3x–2.0x
- Win rate: ~85% (you'll win most rounds)
- Bankroll growth: Slow and steady

#### Balanced Play (Intermediate)

- Target 8–12 reveals per round
- Average multiplier: 2.0x–4.0x
- Win rate: ~60–70%
- Bankroll growth: Moderate, with volatility

#### Aggressive Play (Advanced)

- Target 15+ reveals per round
- Average multiplier: 5.0x–12x+
- Win rate: ~30–40%
- Bankroll growth: Fast but risky; prepare for downswings

## 9.0 Advanced Play: Strategies for Veterans

### The Bankroll Management Framework

Your bankroll is your lifeblood. Protect it.

#### The 1% Rule

- Never bet more than 1% of your total balance in a single round
- Example: 50,000 GEL bankroll → Max 500 GEL per bet
- **Why:** Protects you from catastrophic losses; allows recovery runs

#### The Loss Limit

- Set a daily or session loss limit
- Once you hit it, stop playing
- **Example:** "Today I'll risk 5,000 GEL. If it's gone, I'm done."
- **Why:** Prevents emotional decisions and runaway losses

#### The Win Target

- Set a profit goal and stop when you hit it
- Example: "I'll play until I'm up 2,000 GEL, then I'm done."
- **Why:** Locks in gains before variance turns against you

### Reading the Board (Pattern Recognition)

Some players believe certain patterns influence outcomes. Minescape's randomization is cryptographically fair, but experienced players notice:

- **Hot Streaks:** When you hit 5+ safe reveals in a row, the board "feels" generous. This is confirmation bias, but it's psychologically real.
- **Dead Zones:** Certain grid positions seem unlucky. Again, randomization doesn't work that way—but humans pattern-match.
- **The Comeback:** After a loss, many players feel the next round will compensate. It won't. Each round is independent.

**Pro Tip:** Ignore these patterns. Play mathematically. Bet the odds, not the feels.

### The Optimal Stopping Point

Mathematicians call this "optimal stopping." For Minescape:

#### Expected Value Calculation

**For each reveal, your EV = (Probability Safe) × (Reward) − (Probability Mine) × (Current Payout)**

When EV turns negative, stop playing.

**Example (25-tile grid, 1 mine, after 6 safe reveals):**
- Remaining safe: 18 / 19 total
- Odds next reveal safe: 94.7%
- Multiplier if safe: +0.10x
- Multiplier if mine: -100% (lose everything)

**Math:**
- EV = (0.947 × +0.10x) − (0.053 × current payout)
- EV = +0.0947x − (0.053 × payout)
- If payout is already 2.0x, then EV = +0.0947 − (0.053 × 2.0) = +0.0947 − 0.106 = **negative**

**Conclusion:** At 2.0x on a 1-mine board, the math says cash out, don't risk more.

## 10.0 Risk Management and Responsible Play

### Knowing Your Limits

Minescape is thrilling precisely *because* you control the outcome. But control isn't certainty.

#### Signs of Healthy Play

- ✅ You set limits before each session
- ✅ You cash out when you hit profit goals
- ✅ Losing a round doesn't drive emotional decisions
- ✅ You can walk away mid-session if you choose
- ✅ Gaming is entertainment, not income

#### Warning Signs

- ❌ Chasing losses by increasing bet size
- ❌ Playing longer than planned
- ❌ Betting money you can't afford to lose
- ❌ Feeling stressed or anxious during play
- ❌ Gaming is your primary income source

### The House Edge

Minescape is mathematically fair. But it's designed to favor the operator over time:

- **Player Win Rate:** Roughly 50–65% (varies by strategy)
- **Average Multiplier on Winning Rounds:** 1.5x–3.0x (varies by grid/mines)
- **Long-term Player Edge:** Slightly negative (the house always wins eventually)

**What This Means:** Sustainable profit comes from *excellent bankroll management* and *disciplined play*, not from "getting lucky."

If Minescape stops being fun—if it becomes compulsive—reach out:

## 11.0 Troubleshooting and Common Questions

### "Why did my round end so suddenly?"

You hit a mine. The board revealed it the moment you clicked an unsafe tile. No delays, no hidden mechanics—just the randomized outcome of your choice.

### "Can I predict where the mines are?"

No. Minescape uses cryptographic randomization. Mines are placed after you hit Start, and their positions are genuinely random. No patterns. No tells.

### "What's the best grid size for me?"

- **New to mining games?** Start with 25 tiles, 1 mine.
- **Want higher multipliers?** Jump to 49 tiles, 5 mines.
- **Chasing maximum volatility?** 64 tiles, 10 mines.

There's no "best"—only best for your risk tolerance and bankroll.

### "Is autoplay really safer?"

Autoplay removes emotion. That's great. But it doesn't change the math. Set reasonable auto cash-out targets (2.0x–3.0x) and trust the system.

### "My balance keeps dropping. Am I playing wrong?"

Maybe. Or maybe variance is against you. Even perfect play has losing streaks. If you're losing >20% of your bankroll in a session, tighten your cash-out targets and reduce bet size. Grinding slow beats chasing fast.

### "How do I contact support?"

Menu > Support (in-game), or support@minesscapegames.com. We're here to help.
