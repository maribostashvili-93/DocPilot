# DocPilot UX System Audit - UI Element Inventory

Date: 2026-05-23
Phase: 1 - UX system audit

Scoring uses 1-5 where 5 is strongest. Severity uses P0/P1/P2/P3.

| ID | UI element/controller | Surface | Evidence | Clarity | Efficiency | Consistency | Error prevention | Visual hierarchy | What works | What fails | User impact | Severity | Recommended fix |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| E-01 | Brand logo and primary nav links | Public shell | `AviatorLogo`, landing nav | 4 | 4 | 3 | 3 | 4 | Fast access to products and DocPilot admin. | Aviator brand remains dominant over DocPilot product identity. | Users can confuse demo content with platform name. | P2 | Make DocPilot the platform brand and Aviator a product/workspace example. |
| E-02 | Product cards | Landing | product catalog cards | 4 | 4 | 4 | 3 | 4 | Cards expose product status, docs, version, and description. | Creation CTA is not permission-aware. | Non-admin users may hit dead-end auth. | P3 | Gate creation CTA by login/role state. |
| E-03 | Document cards | Product page, Documents | `DocMiniCard`, document cards | 4 | 4 | 4 | 3 | 4 | Cards summarize type, status, version, owner, date, actions. | Same content appears in different card styles across public/admin. | Information feels familiar but not fully systematized. | P3 | Standardize document card anatomy and action ordering. |
| E-04 | Admin side navigation | Admin shell | `AdminLink` | 4 | 4 | 4 | 3 | 4 | Persistent module navigation is simple. | No settings/workflow route despite product expectations. | Users may assume workflow/settings are missing. | P1 | Add Settings and Workflow/Governance or update product scope copy. |
| E-05 | View headers and header action buttons | Admin pages | `ViewHeader` usage | 4 | 4 | 4 | 3 | 4 | Each page has clear title, subtitle, and primary action. | Header actions can become crowded in translations. | Users scan well but may face dense action clusters. | P3 | Group secondary actions in menu when more than two actions appear. |
| E-06 | KPI cards and progress rows | Dashboard/publishing | `Kpi`, `ProgressRow` | 4 | 4 | 4 | 3 | 4 | Quantitative health is visible and compact. | Metrics are not clickable into filtered worklists. | Users must re-create context manually. | P2 | Make KPIs actionable links with scoped filters. |
| E-07 | Product/document selector dropdowns | Dashboard/documents/translations/sections | native `<select>` controls | 4 | 4 | 4 | 3 | 4 | Native controls are predictable. | Product vs document naming is inconsistent. | Scope errors are possible. | P1 | Use hierarchical selector labels: product, document, doc stream. |
| E-08 | Data tables | Publishing/users | `<table className="data">` | 4 | 4 | 4 | 3 | 4 | Tables fit operational scanning. | No sorting, filtering, column density controls, or bulk operations. | Scale will degrade after prototype seed data. | P2 | Add table controls and saved filters. |
| E-09 | Modal shell | CRUD/screenshot | `Modal` | 4 | 4 | 4 | 3 | 4 | Reusable overlay, close button, and card structure. | Overlay close can discard context unless form-specific guard exists. | Users can lose modal input. | P2 | Add dirty-state handling to all editing modals. |
| E-10 | Warning confirmation card | Destructive actions | `WarningConfirm` | 5 | 4 | 4 | 4 | 5 | Destructive copy is specific and visually distinct. | No typed confirm for high-impact deletion. | Admin mistakes remain possible. | P1 | Add typed confirmation for document deletion and user deletion. |
| E-11 | Toast notifications | Admin shell | `showToast`, `.toast-stack` | 4 | 5 | 4 | 3 | 4 | Immediate feedback after saves/deletes. | Toasts are ephemeral and do not expose undo/action links. | Users may miss important confirmation. | P2 | Add undo/action affordances for reversible changes. |
| E-12 | Generic fields and selects | Forms | `Field`, `Select` | 4 | 5 | 4 | 3 | 4 | Reused form components keep layout consistent. | Required validation is native only; no domain validation. | Invalid slugs, URLs, versions can enter data. | P1 | Add schema validation and inline error messages. |
| E-13 | Login form controls | Admin login | username/password labels | 4 | 5 | 4 | 3 | 4 | Small, accessible labels, autocomplete, required fields. | Prototype credential note is inappropriate for production. | Reduces trust. | P1 | Replace with proper auth and non-sensitive onboarding. |
| E-14 | Document form | Documents | `DocumentForm` | 4 | 4 | 4 | 3 | 4 | Captures core metadata in one grid. | Missing audience, slug, template, navigation, owner assignment model. | Created docs are incomplete for CMS parity. | P1 | Expand into metadata, IA, audience, and template sections. |
| E-15 | Translation product form | Translations | `TranslationProductForm` | 3 | 4 | 3 | 3 | 3 | Allows translation workspace to add/edit doc-like products. | It duplicates document creation with different labels. | Product/document model becomes ambiguous. | P1 | Remove duplicate form or align it to true product/document hierarchy. |
| E-16 | Section action bar | Sections | `DocumentationActionBar` | 3 | 4 | 3 | 3 | 3 | Central controls cover revisions, elements, collapse, publish, draft, edit, duplicate, delete, add. | Too many actions at one level; primary hierarchy is noisy. | Editors may misclick or overlook core action. | P1 | Split into primary save/publish controls and secondary overflow menu. |
| E-17 | Revision buttons | Sections/inline/screenshot/component | `RevisionButtons` | 4 | 5 | 4 | 4 | 4 | Reusable undo/redo controls are easy to recognize. | Scope of undo/redo differs by editor and is not labeled beyond aria label. | Users may undo the wrong layer. | P2 | Display visible scope, e.g. "section undo" vs "marker undo". |
| E-18 | Revision history dropdown | Sections | `documentation-revision-panel` | 4 | 4 | 3 | 4 | 4 | Shows latest/current revision and restore options. | Session/local limitation is not visible. | Users may treat it as durable audit history. | P1 | Label storage scope and implement durable revisions. |
| E-19 | Section controller bar | Sections | `SectionControllerBar` | 3 | 5 | 3 | 3 | 3 | Compact per-section controls give fast edit/publish/reorder/delete. | Icon-only controls require hover/title; symbols are not all standard. | Discoverability and accessibility suffer. | P1 | Use standard icon set, tooltips, and optional text labels in expanded mode. |
| E-20 | Drag handles | Sections/components/markers | section handle, component handles, marker handles | 3 | 4 | 3 | 3 | 3 | Drag handles support direct manipulation. | Keyboard equivalent and hit-target guidance are weak. | Accessibility and precision issues. | P1 | Add keyboard reorder/nudge controls and visible handle focus states. |
| E-21 | Elements library panel | Sections | `ElementsLibraryPanel` | 4 | 4 | 4 | 3 | 4 | Component options include short descriptions. | Drag and click-to-place modes are both present but not explained. | Users can get stuck placing components. | P2 | Add mode instruction and visible insertion placeholders. |
| E-22 | Component builder controls | Section full editor | `component-builder` | 4 | 3 | 4 | 3 | 4 | Supports structured block composition with items/tables. | Dense nested editor lacks validation and preview anchors. | Complex content creation is powerful but tiring. | P2 | Add focused component editor drawer with preview and validation. |
| E-23 | Inline edit overlay button | Sections | `cms-floating-edit-button` | 4 | 5 | 4 | 3 | 5 | Editing target is spatially tied to rendered content. | Hover-only path is hard on touch and keyboard. | Some users cannot discover or operate inline edit. | P1 | Add selected-section edit list and keyboard focus controls. |
| E-24 | ContentEditable rich editor | Inline editor | `contentEditable role=textbox` | 4 | 5 | 3 | 3 | 4 | Edits copy in place and supports paste sanitization. | Browser `execCommand` behavior is inconsistent and deprecated. | Formatting results may vary. | P2 | Move to a supported editor model with schema-based output. |
| E-25 | Rich selection toolbar | Inline editor | `RichSelectionToolbar` | 3 | 4 | 4 | 3 | 3 | Offers bold, italic, code, link, clear, and highlights. | Icon-only labels like `A` and `Tx` are not self-explanatory. | Requires learning and hover title availability. | P2 | Use standard icons with persistent tooltips. |
| E-26 | Screenshot modal | Screenshot editor | `cms-screenshot-modal-card` | 4 | 4 | 4 | 5 | 4 | Full-screen focused editor with dirty-close protection. | Modal contains many panels and scroll regions. | Long marker editing sessions feel heavy. | P2 | Convert to workspace layout with left canvas and right inspector. |
| E-27 | Marker preview canvas | Screenshot editor | `.cms-marker-preview` | 4 | 5 | 4 | 3 | 5 | Direct preview is strong and updates live. | No zoom, snap/grid, or coordinate ruler. | Precision placement is difficult. | P1 | Add zoom, grid, snap guides, and numeric inspector sync. |
| E-28 | Marker resize/rotate handles | Screenshot editor | `MARKER_RESIZE_EDGES` | 3 | 4 | 3 | 2 | 4 | Handles enable resize and pointer rotation. | Handles are aria-hidden and not keyboard-operable. | Accessibility gap and precision problem. | P1 | Add focusable handles or inspector controls for resize/rotation. |
| E-29 | Marker row panels | Screenshot editor | basics/style/link behavior panels | 3 | 3 | 3 | 3 | 3 | Panels separate basics, style, and link behavior. | Shape and hotspot controls share dense patterns with many conditional fields. | Cognitive load is high. | P1 | Use marker-type-specific inspector with collapsed advanced sections. |
| E-30 | Color controls and presets | Marker editor | color inputs, ranges, preset list | 3 | 3 | 4 | 3 | 3 | Swatches and saved presets support reuse. | Color/opacity controls are repeated across border/background/text/dialog/CTA. | Styling can dominate the task. | P2 | Use preset-first workflow and advanced design-token mapping. |
| E-31 | Segmented controls | Marker/component forms | type, align, destination, direction | 4 | 5 | 4 | 3 | 4 | Button groups are faster than dropdowns for small option sets. | Some use generic text and active state only. | Mostly efficient, but needs consistent focus/selected visuals. | P3 | Standardize segmented control component. |
| E-32 | Toggle controls | Translations/markers/components | missing-only, pulse, ordered | 4 | 5 | 3 | 3 | 4 | Binary states are simple. | Implementations vary between checkbox and custom button. | Inconsistent mental model. | P3 | Use one switch/checkbox pattern per domain. |
| E-33 | Translation language rail | Translations | `.language-rail` | 4 | 5 | 4 | 3 | 4 | Language list with progress is highly scannable. | No grouping by status or filters. | 37 languages can become a long scan. | P2 | Add search/status group and pinned active locales. |
| E-34 | Translation key/value rows | Translations | `.key-value-row` | 4 | 4 | 4 | 3 | 4 | Source, label, key, and textarea are visible together. | No per-row validation, save status, comments, or reviewer actions. | Review workflow is incomplete. | P1 | Add row status, comments, dirty/saved indicator, and reviewer assignment. |
| E-35 | Readiness cards | Publishing | `.readiness-card` | 4 | 4 | 4 | 4 | 4 | Release blockers are surfaced before publish. | They do not link to specific remediation targets. | Users spend time finding fixes. | P2 | Make each readiness reason actionable. |
| E-36 | Disabled publish button with title | Publishing | disabled button title | 3 | 4 | 4 | 4 | 3 | Prevents unsafe publishing. | Disabled controls are poor explanation surfaces, especially on touch. | Users may not discover why publish is blocked. | P2 | Show visible blocker list next to each publish action. |
| E-37 | User directory controls | Users | table action buttons | 4 | 4 | 4 | 4 | 4 | CRUD and reset actions are clear. | Role model is too coarse and local-only. | Cannot support enterprise governance. | P0 | Replace with server-backed RBAC and permission matrix. |
| E-38 | Empty states | Public/admin | `EmptyState` and inline empty blocks | 4 | 4 | 4 | 3 | 4 | Empty states include clear next actions. | Some states lack setup sequence and ownership context. | New products still need guidance. | P2 | Use checklists for onboarding empty states. |
| E-39 | Trash icon buttons | Documents/users/sections/components/markers | `.trash-icon` | 3 | 4 | 4 | 4 | 3 | Compact destructive controls are often aria-labeled. | Icon-only destructive actions are close to other frequent actions. | Misclick risk. | P2 | Move destructive actions to overflow or confirm-on-hold for dense bars. |
| E-40 | Back-to-top button | Public reader | `.back-to-top` | 4 | 5 | 4 | 3 | 4 | Useful on long docs. | No text label visible and no section progress. | Minor orientation gap. | P3 | Add active section progress in TOC. |
