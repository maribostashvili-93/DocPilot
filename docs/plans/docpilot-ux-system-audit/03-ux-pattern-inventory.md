# DocPilot UX System Audit - UX Pattern Inventory

Date: 2026-05-23
Phase: 1 - UX system audit

Scoring uses 1-5 where 5 is strongest. Severity uses P0/P1/P2/P3.

| ID | UX pattern | Where used | Evidence | Clarity | Efficiency | Consistency | Error prevention | Visual hierarchy | What works | What fails | User impact | Severity | Recommended fix |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| P-01 | Progressive disclosure | Marker opacity, elements library, revision panel | `showMarkerOpacityControls`, `elementsLibraryOpen`, `showHistory` | 4 | 4 | 3 | 3 | 4 | Keeps some advanced controls out of the default view. | Disclosure is inconsistent: some advanced marker fields remain fully visible. | Users still face high complexity in marker editor. | P1 | Define basic/advanced tiers across marker, component, and HTML editing. |
| P-02 | Modal CRUD flow | Documents, language, release, users | `openModal`, `Modal`, forms | 4 | 4 | 4 | 3 | 4 | Reusable modal pattern is predictable. | Dirty-state protection is not universal. | Form edits can be lost by closing overlay. | P2 | Add a shared dirty-form guard to all modals. |
| P-03 | Destructive confirmation | Delete document/section/user/screenshot | `WarningConfirm` | 5 | 4 | 4 | 4 | 5 | Clear warning copy and impact detail. | High-impact deletes lack typed confirm, archive, or undo. | Admin mistakes can permanently remove local data. | P1 | Add archive/trash and typed confirm for permanent deletion. |
| P-04 | Inline editing | Section rendered content | hover edit button, `contentEditable` | 4 | 5 | 4 | 3 | 5 | Strong context preservation and low mode switching. | Hover dependency and no mobile/keyboard path. | Non-mouse users lose core authoring capability. | P1 | Add keyboard-discoverable edit list and selected-section inspector. |
| P-05 | Full-form editing | Section and metadata forms | `SectionInlineEditor`, `DocumentForm` | 4 | 3 | 3 | 3 | 4 | Useful for structured metadata and full section content. | Duplicates some inline/screenshot controls and exposes raw HTML. | Users must choose between overlapping edit modes. | P1 | Consolidate into one editor model with advanced HTML as guarded mode. |
| P-06 | Direct manipulation | Section drag, component drag, marker drag/resize/rotate | pointer and drag handlers | 3 | 5 | 3 | 2 | 4 | Fast for visual placement and ordering. | Keyboard and precision alternatives are incomplete. | Accessibility and confidence gaps. | P1 | Add keyboard reordering/nudging, grid/snap, and visible coordinates. |
| P-07 | Undo/redo | Document, inline, screenshot, component | `useRevisionedState`, `RevisionButtons` | 4 | 4 | 3 | 4 | 4 | Reversal exists in major editors. | Scopes are different and not durable. | Users can misunderstand recovery guarantees. | P1 | Persist revisions and label scope visibly. |
| P-08 | Revision history/restore | Section action bar | `revisionHistories` | 4 | 4 | 3 | 4 | 4 | History list can restore prior section snapshots. | Local/session semantics are unclear; not tied to publish snapshots. | Audit/compliance trust is overstated. | P0 | Implement durable revision history bound to snapshots and actors. |
| P-09 | Immediate local persistence | Admin state | `localStorage`, `sessionStorage` | 3 | 5 | 3 | 2 | 3 | Prototype feels responsive with no backend dependency. | No sync, audit, conflict handling, backup, or permission enforcement. | Not safe for production CMS work. | P0 | Move core entities to backend persistence with autosave state and audit trail. |
| P-10 | Toast feedback | Saves/deletes/publish | `showToast` | 4 | 5 | 4 | 3 | 4 | Fast confirmation after actions. | No undo links, details, or persistence. | Users may miss consequences of changes. | P2 | Add action toasts with undo/view details where appropriate. |
| P-11 | Disabled guardrails | Publish/readiness/save buttons | disabled states, readiness | 4 | 4 | 4 | 4 | 3 | Prevents release actions before readiness. | Explanations depend on hover/title or separate card. | Touch users may not know why disabled. | P2 | Show visible reason inline with disabled actions. |
| P-12 | Readiness scoring | Publishing | `getReadiness` cards | 4 | 4 | 4 | 4 | 4 | Release quality is summarized before publish. | Readiness is not mapped to workflow approval or immutable snapshots. | Good prototype signal, weak governance. | P0 | Bind readiness to approval gates, content snapshots, and release audit. |
| P-13 | Empty-state guidance | Product/docs/admin pages | empty states | 4 | 4 | 4 | 3 | 4 | Empty states generally include creation CTA. | They do not guide full setup sequence for products/docs/locales/releases. | New product setup remains fragmented. | P2 | Add milestone-style setup checklists. |
| P-14 | Product/document context switching | Dashboard, documents, translations, sections | selects, active doc tabs | 3 | 4 | 3 | 3 | 3 | Users can switch context in each module. | Scope labels are inconsistent and not globally persistent. | Wrong-scope edits are likely as content grows. | P1 | Add global context bar and consistent hierarchy naming. |
| P-15 | Multi-pane workspace | Translations and marker editor | language rail + editor, canvas + inspector | 4 | 5 | 4 | 3 | 4 | Parallel context and editing are efficient. | Marker editor panes are too dense and not clearly prioritized. | Expert workflow is fast; novice workflow is steep. | P2 | Use inspector sections with default collapsed advanced groups. |
| P-16 | Search/filter | Sections/translations | search inputs, missing-only | 4 | 5 | 4 | 3 | 4 | Fast text filtering and missing-only workflow. | Filters are narrow and do not carry from dashboard attention links. | Users repeat search setup. | P2 | Add filterable route state and dashboard deep links. |
| P-17 | Status visualization | Pills/progress bars/KPIs | `Pill`, `ProgressRow`, `Kpi` | 4 | 4 | 4 | 3 | 4 | Status and progress are visible across surfaces. | Status does not always equal actual governance state. | Users may over-trust prototype states. | P1 | Define canonical status model and source of truth. |
| P-18 | Role-based access | Users page/admin guard | `currentUser.role === 'admin'` | 3 | 4 | 3 | 3 | 3 | Users page is restricted to admin. | Permissions are not applied to document, publish, translation, or marker actions. | Client governance is incomplete. | P0 | Implement action-level RBAC across CMS and backend. |
| P-19 | Accessibility labeling | Aria labels, roles | buttons, modals, marker hotspots | 4 | 3 | 3 | 3 | 3 | Many icon controls and hotspots have aria labels. | Drag handles, hidden resize handles, hover-only edit, and custom toggles lack full keyboard semantics. | Accessibility is partial. | P1 | Add keyboard flows, focus management, and semantic control components. |
| P-20 | Error handling | Auth/forms/user CRUD | required fields, thrown errors, toast errors | 3 | 4 | 3 | 3 | 3 | Some validation exists for login and users. | Most content fields rely on native required or no validation. | Data quality issues appear downstream. | P1 | Add schema validation, inline errors, and remediation links. |
| P-21 | Discoverability through iconography | Section controls/text tools | icon-only controls | 3 | 5 | 3 | 3 | 3 | Compact controls preserve editing space. | Several icons are nonstandard or text abbreviations. | New users need trial-and-error. | P2 | Use a standard icon library and tooltips with optional labels. |
| P-22 | Publish as immediate action | Sections/releases | publish buttons | 3 | 5 | 3 | 3 | 3 | Fast publishing in prototype. | Lacks explicit approval, environment, audit, or snapshot semantics. | Enterprise users cannot rely on it. | P0 | Convert to staged publish workflow with review, environment, and rollback. |
